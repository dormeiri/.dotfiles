# Story Circle

Dan Harmon's Story Circle: a character leaves comfort, struggles, and returns changed. In a blog post, the character is usually the author (or the reader).

1. **You:** the starting point. How things were, what you believed.
2. **Need:** something isn't right. A problem, frustration, or curiosity.
3. **Go:** you try something new: a tool, approach, or role.
4. **Search:** it's hard. False starts, adapting, learning.
5. **Find:** the breakthrough, the moment it clicked.
6. **Take:** the cost. What it took, what you gave up, what still doesn't work.
7. **Return:** back to the day-to-day, applying what you learned.
8. **Change:** how you (and the reader) see things differently now.

## Using it

- Steps 1–2 make the opening; 7–8 make the ending. Step 8 should answer the tension set up in step 2.
- Don't label the steps in the post. They shape the flow, they aren't headers.
- Short posts can compress it to: before → disruption → after.
- If there's no shift in understanding, skip the circle and use a plain structure.
