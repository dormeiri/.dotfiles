# Voice and Tone

Write like talking to a peer over coffee: conversational, but with substance.

## Do

- Show the journey, not just the destination
- Admit uncertainty, mistakes, and being wrong
- Use specific, real details: tool names, commands, numbers
- Use inline code naturally for commands and tools
- Mix short and long sentences; use a one-line paragraph for emphasis
- Assume reader knowledge, explain only when needed
- Keep humor sparse and self-aware
- Match tone to section: clear and instructional for technical parts, reflective for personal ones

## Review checklist

- Does it sound like a person talking?
- Is there a clear arc from opening to ending?
- Are technical details specific and accurate?
- Are paragraph lengths varied?
- Does the ending tie back to the opening and leave momentum?
